# Slime Slayers · Update 7 · boss and mini-boss movesets

Every boss and mini-boss has its own moves (no shared or random kits). A move is a wind-up you can read (the pose below, held for its warning time while the telegraph shows on the floor) and then the hits. Bosses have three phases: above half health, below half (faster, new moves), and below a quarter (faster again, their finisher). Mini-bosses add a third move below half health. Generated by `node tools/movesets.js` from `public/config.js`.
